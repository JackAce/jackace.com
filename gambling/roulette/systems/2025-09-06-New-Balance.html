This is a wacky {% include roulette-system-type.html type="Martingale" %} system. When you lose, double up and hit 3x before resetting to the first tier.

Walk when you're up {% include win-loss-amount.html amount=400 %} or more.

You can just make two bets instead of the 4 bets to simplify things. You can bet $30 on high and $20 on the 1st Dozen instead. And double up the bets for the other tiers.

I don't really like {% include roulette-system-type.html type="Martingale" %} systems in general...but they're ESPECIALLY bad when you have to win SEVERAL times at the higher tier to recover.
