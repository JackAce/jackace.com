I...really don't understand this system. I think it's supposed to be a Multi-Leg version of {% include roulette-system-link.html reviewUrl="2022-09-12-Ride-or-Die" -%}, but it's just...awful.

I think you're supposed to parlay wins for hits, but for some reason they're betting $20 on a Dozen/Column instead of $15. So that means you parlay AND you add additional money to the bet.

I think it's stupid to parlay a bet AND add on to it. That means it's possible to win and not have enough money for the next bet. I hate that.
