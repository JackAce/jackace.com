This seems like such a goofy system. You're using Fibonacci on your losses, but you're not recovering from previous losses. That's because you're spreading yourself thin. Fibonacci works best with a Dozen (2:1) bet, not betting on 26 numbers.

If you want to consolidate your bets, you can just bet $30 on two Dozens or Columns and then $10 on an uncovered Split bet.
