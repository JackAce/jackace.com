This is a goofy {% include roulette-system-type.html type="Positive Progression" %} system with very weird betting patterns.

It starts out innocent enough, with two equally sized Dozen bets, but then it goes wacky after that, with a lobsided $20 and $70 Dozen bets (yo, wtf?)

I don't like these goofy hedging bets. You're spread too thin.
