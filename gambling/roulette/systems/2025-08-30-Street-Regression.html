This is a goofy {% include roulette-system-type.html type="Negative Progression" %} system where you bet smaller amounts when you win (?) and bigger amounts when you lose (?).

If you win on the first tier, go to tier 2 ($20). You pull back your winning bet, and chop your bets in half. If you win again, just pull back the winning bet and bet $15 (tier 3). If that hits, go to tier 4, which is two double street bets of $10 each.

Walk when you complete the cycle (winning on all four tiers).

I don't like the nature of this system...You're betting smaller, but really reducing the liklihood of completing the cycle. I suppose that means you can last longer at the tables, but you're just churning bets and grinding out the house edge. I don't like that.
