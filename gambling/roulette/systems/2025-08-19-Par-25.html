This is a goofy {% include roulette-system-type.html type="Positive Progression" %} with goofy betting patterns that can be optimized.

When you win, press $5 (one unit) on each of the 5 bets. Walk when you're up {% include win-loss-amount.html amount=180 -%}.

You can consolidate the Double Street bets quite a bit. For tier 1, you can just add $15 to the 19-36 bet, and replace the two $10 Double Streets with a single $10 Dozen bet. When you press, you can add another $15 and $10 to the same spots.
